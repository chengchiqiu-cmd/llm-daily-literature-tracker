中文摘要（翻译）

研究表明，绿色金融会影响碳排放，并在可持续发展中发挥普遍作用；然而，关于绿色金融对更广泛气候脆弱性的影响，尤其是生成式人工智能（Generative Artificial Intelligence，GenAI）的调节作用，相关研究仍然十分有限。因此，本研究考察了2000—2024年G7经济体中绿色金融对气候脆弱性的非线性影响，以及GenAI的调节作用。为确保结果可靠且政策启示具有充分依据，研究采用稳健且相关的方法处理横截面依赖、非平稳性和分布异质性问题。结合动态能力和环境库兹涅茨曲线（Environmental Kuznets Curve，EKC）假说在研究框架中的作用，研究结果表明，绿色金融与气候脆弱性之间存在非线性关系，证实了倒U形关系。在早期阶段，绿色金融可能会增加脆弱性；但达到阈值后，绿色金融有助于降低脆弱性。GenAI通过提高绿色金融的效率，减弱了这种早期不利影响，帮助经济体在较低的绿色金融水平下实现气候韧性。此外，ESG准备度、能源生产率和制度对气候韧性发挥有利作用，而城市化和收入则不利于气候韧性和适应目标。G7经济体的政策制定者可以考虑采取行动，支持包括GenAI在内的现代技术，扩大绿色金融机制，提高企业准备度，强化制度及其能力建设，并改善能源状况，从而有效降低气候脆弱性，实现可持续经济增长并构建气候韧性。

英文原摘要

Research has shown that green finance has an impact on carbon emissions and a pervasive role in sustainable development; yet studies on the green finance impact on broader climate vulnerability remain scarce, particularly regarding the moderating role of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI). Therefore, this study investigates the non-linear impact of green finance on climate vulnerability and the moderating role of GenAI within G7 economies from 2000-2024. To ensure that the results are reliable and policy insights are well-informed, cross-sectional dependency, non-stationarity and distributional heterogeneity are addressed using robust and relevant methods. Considering the role of dynamic capabilities and the EKC hypothesis in the framework, our findings indicate that green finance has a non-linear relationship with climate vulnerability, confirming inverted U-shape. In the early stages, green finance may increase vulnerability, but upon reaching the threshold, it helps reduce vulnerability. GenAI weakens this early negative effect by improving the efficiency of green finance, helping economies achieve climate resilience at a lower level of green finance. Moreover, ESG readiness, energy productivity and institutions play a favorable role for climate resilience while urbanization, and income play a detrimental role in climate resilience and adaptation objectives. Policy makers in the G7 economies may reflect on executing actions to support modern technologies including GenAI, expanded green financial mechanisms, enhancing business readiness, strengthening institutions and their capacity building, and energy improvements to effectively reduce climate vulnerability, achieve sustainable economic growth, and build climate resilience.
