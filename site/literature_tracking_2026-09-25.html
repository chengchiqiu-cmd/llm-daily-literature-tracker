英文原摘要

This paper examines the regulatory design of artificial intelligence (AI) in Chile through the lens of adaptive regulation theory and dynamic risk governance. Using a comparative institutional analysis of five cases —the European Union, the United States, the United Kingdom, Singapore and the Chilean AI bill (consolidated Bills No. 16.821-19 and 15.869-19)—, with information current as of 16 December 2025, three design dimensions are ordinally coded: robustness of normative principles (P), technical translation capacity (T) and bureaucratic friction (F), combined in a heuristic regulatory effectiveness function E = (P × T) / F. Three findings emerge: (1) the cases converge on principles, and differences in expected effectiveness are driven by T and F; (2) in Chile, friction stems less from the AI law than from its overlap with four adjacent compliance regimes; and (3) technical translation capacity is the binding constraint of the Chilean design, since risk self-classification is only credible under ex post verification, and the text approved at first reading provides for infringements and fines but not for the authority, the procedure or the metrics needed to enforce them. Policy implications follow, in order of priority: enabling enforcement through an expressly empowered authority and procedure; equipping it with a technical risk taxonomy and verifiable metrics under online monitoring; and formalising inter-agency coordination, while retaining divergence from the European model in implementation mechanisms. The study is qualitative and exploratory; its propositions are stated as testable hypotheses. 中文摘要（翻译）

建筑物中的HVAC系统在能源使用和运营碳排放方面发挥着重要作用,使得可靠的故障检测和诊断对其有效运行和乘客安全至关重要.然而,传统方法往往面临可扩展性挑战,因为它们需要特定地点的规则,大标签数据集或有限的可解释模型. 在本研究中,我们介绍了一个自动化故障检测和诊断工作流程,将一个大型语言模型与语义和知识图集成起来.拟议的框架使用一个语义元数据模型来表示HVAC系统拓学,一个导向的因果图来编码物理预期的因果关系,以及预先计算的统计证据来指导基于大型语言模型的诊断推断. 而不是直接从原始传感器数据推理,LLM基于结构化系统背景和数量证据的故障假设.我们通过使用ASHRAE指南36的空气侧节省器序列运行的单区变量空气量空气处理装置验证了实际控制环境室的工作流程. 我们包括了三种代表性故障:一个被过置的供应风扇变量速度驱动器,一个卡住的户外空气门器和一个漂移的供应空气温度传感器.结果表明,拟议的工作流可以通过解释跨组件相互作用和故障传播模式来识别主要故障. 通过在系统解剖学,专家知识和统计证据方面的LLM推理,这种方法提高了诊断透明度,降低了幻觉风险,并为人工智能辅助的HVAC故障诊断提供了可扩展的途径.

英文原摘要

HVAC systems in buildings play a major role in energy use and operational carbon emissions, making reliable fault detection and diagnostics important for their efficient operation and occupant safety. However, conventional methods often face scalability challenges because they require site-specific rules, large labeled datasets, or limitedly interpretable models. In this study, we present an automated fault detection and diagnostic workflow that integrates a large language model with semantic and knowledge graphs. The proposed framework uses a semantic metadata model to represent HVAC system topology, a directed causal graph to encode physics-expected cause-and-effect relationships, and precomputed statistical evidence to guide large language model-based diagnostic inference. Rather than reasoning directly from raw sensor data, the LLM generates fault hypotheses grounded in a structured system context and quantitative evidence. We validated the workflow in a real controlled environmental chamber using a single-zone variable-air-volume air-handling unit operating under an ASHRAE Guideline 36 air-side economizer sequence. We included three representative faults: an overridden supply fan variablespeed drive, a stuck outdoor air damper, and a drifted supply air temperature sensor. Results show that the proposed workflow can identify the primary faults by interpreting cross-component interactions and fault propagation patterns. By grounding LLM reasoning in system ontology, expert knowledge, and statistical evidence, this approach improves diagnostic transparency, reduces hallucination risk, and provides a scalable pathway for AI-assisted HVAC fault diagnosis.
