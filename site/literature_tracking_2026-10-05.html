中文摘要（翻译）

研究表明,在敏捷项目中应用人工智能 (AI) 带来了风险识别,后期管理,冲刺规划和资源配置.然而,向每个配送团队成员提供生成人工智能许可的组织并没有更快地提供软件.本文探讨了这个问题的原因.缺陷并不是来自模型. 公司将生成人工智能交给开发人员,测试人员,建筑师,产品所有者和 Scrum 师,他们将其作为个人助理,从而限制能力.交付决策背后的分析转移到任何评论员都无法检查的私人对话. 此外,当一个生成工具在许多小步骤中连续使用时,该工具建立了该项目的稳定的工作内存,并返回了精确引用的材料. 为了解决这些问题,我们提出AI软件工厂:一个角色特定的代理的生产线,在使用前设计和委托,在定义点检查,并由合格的人在冲刺边界而不是每次交付审查. 经验人员由专家的快速工程师撰写,评审人员判断出口,他们的发现作为版本的工程变化返回了经验者作者.

英文原摘要

Research suggests applying Artificial intelligence (AI) for agile projects results in risk identification, backlog management, sprint planning and resource allocation. Yet organizations that provided a generative AI license to every member of a delivery team are not delivering software faster. This paper explores the reason for this problem. The shortfall does not come from the models. It follows from how the tools were introduced.,Firms hand generative AI to developers, testers, architects, product owners and scrum masters, who use it as a personal assistant thereby capping the capability. The analysis behind delivery decisions migrates into private conversations that no reviewer can inspect. Further, when a generative tool is used continuously across many small steps, the tool builds a stable working memory of the project and returns well-referenced material. Once the application is live and a defect appears, the tool cannot reliably diagnose work its own reasoning produced, and blame attaches to a tool.,To address these issues, we propose AI software factory: a production line of role-specific Agents, designed and commissioned before use, inspected at defined points, and reviewed by qualified humans at the sprint boundary rather than at every handoff. Agents are written by expert prompt engineers, reviewers judge outputs, and their findings return to the Agent’s author as versioned engineering change.
