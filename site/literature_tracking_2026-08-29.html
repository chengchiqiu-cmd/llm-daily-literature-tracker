中文摘要（翻译）

在这种情况下,人类运营商可以对系统的信任比与人类行动结合使用的系统的准确性更重要,这些系统被称为人类和人工智能 (AI) 系统.这些系统以它们对联合性能 (过度信任和低信任) 的影响和发展安全关键故障的风险来评估. 这项审查集中在一种新兴的信任校准技术上,该技术集成了两个互补类型的数据:持续的非自愿生理数据 (EEG,EDA,ET,HRV和fNIRS) 捕捉用户的默默的心理过程 (认知和情感状态),以及大语言模型 (LLM) 所产生的自然语言解释,反映了该模型的推理和不确定性. 我们编制了2019年至2025年的52篇同行评审,最近发表的信任自动化论文,这些论文通常是单独开发的,以总结生理传感技术的最新状态,并概述实际的多模式闭环校准架构的概念和技术解决方案. 该审查建立了一个概念框架,将生理感觉方面与域和LLM解释生成行为信号联系起来,总结了生理信任分类准确性与不确定性通信策略的比较证据,并指出了方法缺口,如缺乏生态验证,生理环境中透程度 证据,跨领域的通用性以及基于LLM的解释生成标准校准措施的缺乏. 最后,我们解释了一些关于产生动态,实时信任校准的影响,作为吸引力值校准的潜在有前途,但在很大程度上未经审查的途径,并建议实验性测试的一些优先领域,包括要求最小的物理设备和可接受的校准错误在"关键应用"和可能的纵向实地研究.

英文原摘要

In such cases, the trust that can be placed in the system by the human operator is more important than the accuracy of the system used in combination with human actions, which are referred to as human and artificial intelligence (AI) systems. These are assessed in terms of their effect on joint performance (overtrust and undertrust), and their risk to developing safety-critical failures. This review concentrates on an emerging technique of trust calibration which integrates two complementary types of data: the continuous involuntary physiological data (EEG, EDA, ET, HRV and fNIRS) capturing the tacit psychological processes of a user (cognition and affective state), and the natural-language explanations generated by the large language model (LLM) reflecting the model's reasoning and uncertainty. We curate 52 peer-reviewed recently published papers from 2019 to 2025 and seminal papers on the subject of trust-in-automation, which are usually developed separately, to summarise the current state of the art of physiological sensing, and outline concepts and technical solutions for an actual multimodal closed-loop calibration architecture. The review establishes a conceptual framework that connects physiological sensing aspects with behavioural signals in domain and LLM explanation generation, summarizes comparative evidence on physiological trust-classification accuracy along with uncertainty-communication strategies for LLM, and points out methodological gaps, such as the lack of ecological validation, the degree of obtrusiveness in physiological measurements, cross-domain generalisability and absence of standard measures for calibration in LLm-based explanation generation. Lastly, we explain some implications with regard to the generation of dynamic, real-time trust calibration as a potentially promising, but largely unexamined avenue of attraction value calibration, and suggest some priority areas for empirical testing, including mandated minimum physical equipment and acceptable calibration error in "critical application" and possible longitudinal field studies.

最近的LLM代理的进步使得资产定价的新范式成为可能,我们称之为代理实验性资产定价 (AEAP):系统自主地进行科学发现过程.我们定义AEAP并确定其核心构建块.现有的评估实践仅对输出因素或交易进行后检,而不是产生它们的自主发现系统. 我们专注于因子发现,提供参考架构,对发现的因子进行严格的评估标准, 作为该架构的具体例子,我们使用这个标准对两家美国股权板进行了5个重新实施的基线进行了SEADS评估:没有一个指标一致地排名系统,同时激励了多个轴的评估. 我们还报告了对未来AEAP系统的进一步评估陷的负面发现和局限性.

英文原摘要

Recent advances in LLM agents enable a new paradigm for asset pricing, which we call Agentic Empirical Asset Pricing (AEAP): systems that autonomously conduct the scientific discovery process itself. We define AEAP and identify its core building blocks. Existing evaluation practices backtest only the outputs—factors or trades—not the autonomous discovery system that produced them. We focus on factor discovery, contributing a reference architecture, a rigorous evaluation standard for discovered factors, and a method for out-of-sample backtesting the discovery system. As a concrete instance of that architecture, we evaluate SEADS against five re-implemented baselines on two US equity panels using this standard: no single metric ranks the systems consistently, motivating evaluation on multiple axes at once. A separate rolling re-execution then asks the complementary question of whether the discovery process itself, not one static output, is reliable. We also report negative findings and limitations that surface further evaluation pitfalls for future AEAP systems.

我们研究生成人工智能 (GenAI) 如何重塑CEO薪酬合同.利用ChatGPT的公开发布和企业前冲击GenAI曝光的跨部门变化,我们发现GenAI增加CEO薪酬,主要是通过基于股权的薪酬.技术熟练的董事会和更具竞争力的CEO劳动力市场的影响更强大. 基于冲击的仪器变量设计将劳动力成本降低和公司不确定性增加确定为GenAI推动补偿重新设计的两个道.GenAI引发的劳动力流离失所创造了余额,引起了机构的担忧,而更大的不确定性增加了长期风险承担激励的需求. 根据这些合同需求,公司将首席执行官的薪酬与更长期的绩效联系起来,减少对短期会计措施的依赖,并增加首席执行官达尔塔和维加.通过两种道的补偿调整预测未来公司价值将会更高,并促进更高效的内部资源分配. 总体而言,我们的发现支持最佳的合同,而不是管理层的租金提取,并突出补偿重新设计,作为一个促进企业有效地适应GenAI的治理机制.

英文原摘要

We study how generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) reshapes CEO compensation contracts. Exploiting the public release of ChatGPT and cross-sectional variation in firms' pre-shock GenAI exposure, we find that GenAI increases CEO pay, primarily through equity-based compensation. The effects are stronger with tech-savvy boards and in more competitive CEO labor markets. A shock-based instrumental variables design identifies labor cost reductions and increased firm uncertainty as two channels through which GenAI drives compensation redesign. GenAI-induced labor displacement creates surplus that raises agency concerns, while greater uncertainty increases the need for long-term risk-taking incentives. Consistent with these contracting needs, firms tie CEO pay to longer-horizon performance, reduce reliance on short-term accounting measures, and increase CEO Delta and Vega. Compensation adjustments through both channels predict higher future firm value and facilitate more efficient internal resource allocation. The results are robust to alternative specifications and tests distinguishing GenAI from broader labor-saving technologies. Overall, our findings support optimal contracting rather than managerial rent extraction and highlight compensation redesign as a governance mechanism that facilitates firms' efficient adaptation to GenAI.
