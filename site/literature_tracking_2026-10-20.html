随着人工智能的成熟,产生的市场将会有多大的竞争力?使用包括1.74亿消费者和1.14亿交易的新型交易级信用和借记卡数据,我们研究了付费服务的采用以及该市场的需求侧竞争基础.截至2026年4月,约2.9%的美国人 消费者支付至少一个主要的生成人工智能服务,年轻和收入较高的消费者中采用率最高.几乎所有这些付费用户都会订阅每月30美元或更低的基本计划.三个模式表明有限的差异化和更换成本较低. 其次,各大AI供应商的年度客户保留率在20%至45%之间,低于许多其他订阅产品.第三,转换在主要模型发布中增加.这些发现激励我们开发和估计人工智能服务需求和供应的结构模型,显示出强大的竞争压力. 消费者对价格非常敏感,对特定供应商的偏好比整体人工智能更弱,并且将人工智能服务视为越来越接近的替代品. 我们的结果表明,由于AI模型质量差异化没有大幅增加,服务之间的更换性将会限制供应商的定价能力,并使消费者能够占据这些服务产生的余的很大一部分.

英文原摘要

How competitive will the market for generative AI be as it matures? Using novel transaction-level credit and debit card data covering 174 million consumers and 114 billion transactions, we study the adoption of paid services and the demand-side foundations of competition in this market. As of April 2026, approximately 2.9% of U.S. consumers pay for at least one major generative AI service, with adoption highest among younger and higher-income consumers. Nearly all of these paid users subscribe to basic plans that cost $30 or less per month. Three patterns suggest limited differentiation and low switching costs. First, although multihoming is common, it has declined as providers' capabilities have expanded. Second, annual customer retention rates range from 20% to 45% across major AI providers, lower than those for many other subscription products. Third, switching rises around major model releases. Motivated by these findings, we develop and estimate a structural model of AI service demand and supply that reveals strong competitive pressure. Consumers are highly price sensitive, have much weaker preferences for particular providers than for AI overall, and view AI services as increasingly close substitutes. Our results suggest that, absent a substantial increase in AI model quality differentiation, growing substitutability among services will constrain providers' pricing power and allow consumers to capture a large share of the surplus generated by these services.

英文原摘要

Complex manufacturing systems require scheduling decision-support methods that can adapt to stochastic disruptions while preserving operational interpretability and reusable domain knowledge. Existing approaches remain limited in different ways. Dispatching rules are fast and interpretable but myopic, reinforcement learning methods are adaptive but often black-box and costly to retrain, and genetic programming can generate heuristics but suffers from inefficient and unguided search. Recent studies have attempted to leverage the reasoning capabilities of large language models (LLMs) to solve scheduling problems. However, these approaches remain constrained by weak search guidance, insufficient grounding in manufacturing-specific knowledge, and limited mechanisms for reusing accumulated evolutionary experience. To address these limitations, this study proposes knowledge-guided evolutionary program search (KEPS), an LLM-based automated heuristic design framework that combines evolutionary program search with retrieval-augmented knowledge guidance to generate interpretable heuristics for manufacturing scheduling under stochastic machine breakdowns and complex operational constraints. KEPS frames heuristic evolution as a knowledge-accumulating search process that retrieves relevant domain knowledge, generates offspring heuristics, analyzes parent--offspring behavioral differences through expert-designed comparative analysis procedures, distills effective strategies and failure patterns, and reuses the accumulated knowledge to guide subsequent generations. Experiments across static, dynamic, and high-fidelity semiconductor manufacturing simulation environments demonstrate that KEPS achieves strong and robust performance as problem complexity and operational uncertainty increase. These results suggest that knowledge-guided LLM-based heuristic generation can support effective and interpretable scheduling under practical manufacturing constraints.

英文原摘要

Research on generative AI has examined why people adopt and keep using these tools, but it usually treats use as undifferentiated and measures generic usefulness or satisfaction. We ask instead what users experience as the communicative payoff of use. Drawing on uses and gratifications and communication competence theory, we introduce AI-Mediated Communication Purpose (AIMCP), spanning professional, knowledge-seeking, creative, multilingual, and social/relational purposes, and develop perceived communication effectiveness (PCE) as the experiential outcome. A quota-based online panel survey of 1,022 Taiwanese generative AI users was analyzed with composite-based structural models and extensive robustness checks. Professional and multilingual purposes were the strongest predictors of PCE, and the multilingual association was concentrated among users who interact with AI in English; knowledge-seeking use showed no association. PCE was the strongest correlate of continued-use intention and carried part of the association between performance expectancy, AI self-efficacy, and intention. Adding PCE to an expectancy-and-efficacy model raised out-of-sample explained variance in intention from .34 to .49. Overall intensity of purposive use, rather than the breadth of purposes, was related to intention directly. The findings place experienced communicative payoff, differentiated by purpose, at the center of sustained generative AI use.
