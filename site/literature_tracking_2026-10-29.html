中文摘要（翻译）

人工智能采用可以提高个人预测精度,同时减少交易者之间的信息多样性. 我们在两阶段的凯尔类型市场中研究这种交易,交易者在交易前选择独特的人类信号和更精确的共享 AI信号. 在双交易者和连续模式中,我们确定了人工智能完全采用的条件,因为独特的平衡,但与没有人工智能采用相比,对战略交易者带来较低的预期收益.这种人工智能单种植悖论是因为常见的预测错误能够存活集成并同步订单流动,使人群超过更高精度的收益. 在连续悖论制度中,通过一次性折扣征收AI采用税收,可以将个人激励与集体利最大化调整起来,使规划者的最佳采用率成为独特的去中心化平衡. 使用理论模型作为基准,我们将信息获取决策委托给LLM代理人,在三组实验中检查了最佳反应,对他人的信念和沟通.当其他交易者采用率被给定时,代理人可以解决个人优化问题并选择最大化利的信号. 在对别人的行为形成自己的信念时,部分采用设置中的代理人往往期望人工智能采用超过平衡率.然后他们选择人类信号以避免预期的拥挤,从而导致实际采用低于平衡.允许沟通时,即使在理论预测部分采用的情况下,完全采用也会出现;代理人也试图为私人利益误导他人.

英文原摘要

AI adoption can improve individual forecast precision while reducing the diversity of information across traders. We study this trade-off in a two-stage Kyle-type market where traders choose between idiosyncratic human signals and a more precise shared AI signal before trading. In both two-trader and continuum models, we identify conditions under which full AI adoption arises as the unique equilibrium yet yields lower combined expected profits for strategic traders compared to no AI adoption. This AI monoculture paradox arises because common forecast errors survive aggregation and synchronize order flow, allowing crowding to outweigh the gains from greater precision. In the continuum paradox regime, taxing AI adoption and returning the tax revenue through lump-sum rebates can align individual incentives with collective profit maximization, making the planner’s optimal adoption rate the unique decentralized equilibrium. Using the theoretical model as a benchmark, we delegate information acquisition decisions to LLM agents in three sets of experiments examining best responses, beliefs about others, and communication. When other traders’ adoption rate is given, agents can solve the individual optimization problem and choose the profit-maximizing signal. When forming their own beliefs about others’ behavior, agents in partial-adoption settings often expect AI adoption to exceed the equilibrium rate. They then choose human signals to avoid anticipated crowding, driving actual adoption below equilibrium. With communication allowed, full adoption emerges even in a setting where theory predicts partial adoption; agents also attempt to mislead others for private gain.
