中文摘要（翻译）

自2022年底以来,像ChatGPT和Claude这样的生成人工智能工具迅速成为学习,工作和决策的日常支持. 这篇叙事评论汇总了2022年至2026年发布的经验研究,系统性评论和有关依赖模式,认知和心理后果以及背景因素的主要机构报告. 它映射了在教育,工作场所和决策环境中所知之处;确定了包括概念碎片化,横断设计的占主导地位和有限的纵向证据在内的主要差距;并探讨了为什么印度尽管在绝对方面是最大的用户人口之一, 开放AI,人类学和印度高等教育报告的高采用数据与认知债务,技能缩,决策质量和针对印度教育和劳动力市场条件的心理社会影响的稀有严格证据共存.

英文原摘要

Generative artificial intelligence tools such as ChatGPT and Claude have rapidly become everyday supports for studying, working, and decision-making since late 2022. This narrative review synthesises published empirical studies, systematic reviews, and major institutional reports from 2022 to 2026 on patterns of reliance, cognitive and psychological consequences, and contextual factors. It maps what is known across educational, workplace, and decision contexts; identifies major gaps including conceptual fragmentation, the predominance of cross-sectional designs, and limited longitudinal evidence; and examines why India, despite being one of the largest user populations in absolute terms, remains under-studied in peer-reviewed research on dependence and over-reliance. High adoption data from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Indian higher-education reports coexist with sparse rigorous evidence on cognitive debt, skill atrophy, decision quality, and psychosocial effects specific to Indian educational and labour-market conditions. The review positions Indian research as a high-priority gap for sociological inquiry into agency, institutional pressure, inequality, and technological mediation.
