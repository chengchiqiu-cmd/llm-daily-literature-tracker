中文摘要（翻译）

人工智能助理和生成的人工智能系统正在从面向人类的知识管理过程转移到自动化,代理介导的数字经济. 随着知识被自主代理人发现,评估,总结,获取,保留,重新组合和重复使用在平台上,流通可以比原产地验证,访问控制,使用属性,结算和信任机制可以更新更快. 本工作论文将人工智能介导的知识市场概念化为可管理的数字经济体,并提出了一个概念化和设计导向的结构架构,该架构结合了来源,访问,定居和系统性信任.核心单元是验证的知识使用事件:一个可观测,可接受和可定居的访问或重复使用的有限单元. 这篇论文没有提出特定的区块链协议,定价算法或密码原始;它提供了可互操作知识交换的协议面向治理架构. 它通过工作场景展示了设计逻辑,并概述了选择延迟,来源覆盖率,审计完整性,争端率,信任降低和政策后泄漏等诊断,以评估知识流通是否在加强下仍然可治理. 该贡献是架构和概念性的;它不经验地评估部署的市场,并且旨在支持后期基于场景的分析,重复模拟,对抗性压力测试和案例比较.

英文原摘要

AI assistants and generative AI systems are shifting knowledge creation, retention, and use from human-facing knowledge management processes toward automated, agent-mediated digital economies. As knowledge is discovered, evaluated, summarized, acquired, retained, recombined, and reused across platforms by autonomous agents, circulation can be amplified faster than provenance verification, access control, usage attribution, settlement, and trust mechanisms can update. This working paper conceptualizes AI-mediated knowledge markets as governable digital economies and proposes a conceptual and design-oriented structural architecture that couples provenance, access, settlement, and systemic trust. The central unit is the validated knowledge usage event: a bounded unit of access or reuse that is observable, admissible, and settleable. The paper does not propose a specific blockchain protocol, pricing algorithm, or cryptographic primitive; it offers a protocol-facing governance architecture for interoperable knowledge exchange. It demonstrates the design logic through a worked scenario and outlines diagnostics such as selection latency, provenance coverage, audit completeness, dispute rate, trust degradation, and post-policy leakage for assessing whether knowledge circulation remains governable under reinforcement. The contribution is architectural and conceptual; it does not empirically evaluate a deployed market, and it is intended to support later scenario-based analysis, replay simulation, adversarial stress testing, and case comparison.
