### 中文摘要（翻译）

这份临时论文评估了大曼彻斯特十个地区的人工智能劳动力替代风险.它将人工智能职业和工业暴露指数应用于2021年人口普查和公司之家数据,并通过LLM协助估计,将主要增加或取代每个部门的工作,并对生态系统密度进行基于地点的调整. 城市地区大部分劳动力都处于增强区,但仅仅在替代门之上,并且暴露在各个地区之间和地区内有所不同.在拥有四名或多名员工的企业中,人工智能暴露率较高的地区显示出更低的增长概率,与英国范围内的模式相反,并与贝森对技术的解释一致,最终降低了劳动力需求. 微型企业遵循国家模式.供应商向成本恢复定价的举动增加了对依赖补贴人工智能的组织的进一步风险.这篇论文提出了十一项建议,以定价弹性,市级政策,早期职业管道,并将人工智能扩展到最有可能增加员工的职业.

### 英文原摘要

This interim paper assesses AI labour replacement risk across Greater Manchester's ten boroughs. It applies AI occupational and industry exposure indices to Census 2021 and Companies House data, with LLM-assisted estimates of whether AI will mainly augment or replace work in each sector and a place-based adjustment for ecosystem density. Most of the city-region's workforce sits in the augmentation zone, but only just above the substitution threshold, and exposure varies between and within boroughs. Among firms with four or more employees, boroughs with higher AI exposure show lower augmentation probabilities, the reverse of the UK-wide pattern and consistent with Bessen's account of technology eventually reducing labour demand. Microbusinesses follow the national pattern. Vendors' moves towards cost-recovery pricing add a further risk for organisations dependent on subsidised AI. The paper makes eleven recommendations, centred on pricing resilience, borough-level policy, early-career pipelines, and extending AI to occupations where it is most likely to augment workers.
