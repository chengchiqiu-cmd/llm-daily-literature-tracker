### 中文摘要（翻译）

数字微保险可以扩大获得财务保护的机会,但保险人可能无法因为不规则的收入,支付摩擦,有限的产品理解或获得及时援助而续约. 他们也可能会引入风险,包括不准确的解释,隐私问题,自动化偏见和被视为强制性的消息.本文开发了一个为拟议的随机控制试验 (RCT) 的协议,以评估生成人工智能聊天机是否可以改善数字微保险的政策保留. 拟议的设计将通过聊天机器人支持的续约通信与接近续约或贡献期限的符合条件的保险人之间的标准数字通信进行比较.主要结果是在预先指定的后续点上客观记录保留;次要结果包括支付及时性,理解性,服务解决,信任,投诉和退出率. 随机化,分配隐藏,治疗意图分析和消费者保障措施已详述. 没有提供参与者级实验数据集,因此本文没有报告完成的试验结果. 相反,它提出了一项可复制的协议和分析计划,以便在伦理审查和数据访问批准后实施,与加纳,尼日利亚和其他数字微保险市场相关.

### 英文原摘要

Digital microinsurance can widen access to financial protection, yet policyholders may fail to renew because of irregular income, payment friction, limited product understanding, or difficulty obtaining timely assistance. Generative artificial intelligence (AI) chatbots may reduce some barriers by explaining policy terms, answering questions conversationally, and providing timely reminders. They may also introduce risks, including inaccurate explanations, privacy concerns, automation bias, and messages perceived as coercive. This paper develops a protocol for a proposed randomised controlled trial (RCT) to evaluate whether a generative AI chatbot improves policy retention in digital microinsurance. The proposed design compares chatbot-supported renewal communication with standard digital communication among eligible policyholders approaching a renewal or contribution deadline. The primary outcome is objectively recorded retention at a prespecified follow-up point; secondary outcomes include payment timeliness, comprehension, service resolution, trust, complaints, and opt-out rates. Randomisation, allocation concealment, intention-to-treat analysis, and consumer safeguards are specified. No participant-level experimental dataset was supplied, so this paper does not report completed trial findings. Instead, it presents a reproducible protocol and analysis plan for implementation following ethics review and data access approval, with relevance to Ghana, Nigeria, and other digital microinsurance markets.
