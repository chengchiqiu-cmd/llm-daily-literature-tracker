### 中文摘要（翻译）

随着人工智能生成平台开始通过广告钱,一个重要问题是广告是否以及如何影响用户参与.本文提供了美国ChatGPT广告引入的早期证据. 采用地铁和国家级移动应用使用数据,我们检查了ChatGPT广告引入后活跃用户数量的变化.与控制国家相比,与美国的差异差异估计显示,在广告推出后,每周活跃用户的ChatGPT数量下降了5.4%. 异质性分析表明,在拥有更大的 AI 应用用户基础的都市地区,这种下降更明显.与竞争的生成 AI 应用程序,即克劳德和双胞胎相比,也产生了类似的负面相对估计.在国家层面上,我们还发现在美国在ChatGPT和订阅收入上花费的时间相对下降. 虽然我们的分析不考虑广告收入,因此无法评估广告推广的整体利能力,但结果突出了广告利和生成人工智能平台的用户参与之间的潜在权衡.

### 英文原摘要

As generative AI platforms begin to monetize through advertising, an important question is whether and how advertisements affect user engagement. This paper provides early evidence from the introduction of advertisements on ChatGPT in the United States. Using metro-level and country-level mobile application usage data, we examine changes in the number of active users following the introduction of advertising on ChatGPT. Difference-indifferences estimates comparing the United States with control countries show a 5.4% decline in ChatGPT weekly active users following the launch of advertisements. Heterogeneity analyses indicate that the decline is more pronounced in metropolitan areas with larger pre-existing AI app user bases. Comparisons with competing generative AI applications, namely Claude and Gemini, yield similarly negative relative estimates. At the country level, we also find relative declines in time spent on ChatGPT and subscription revenue in the United States. While our analysis does not account for advertising revenue and therefore cannot assess the overall profitability of introducing ads, the findings highlight a potential trade-off between advertising monetization and user engagement for generative AI platforms. 1
