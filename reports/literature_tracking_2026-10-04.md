### 中文摘要（翻译）

公共大型语言模型API目录在异质货币,标识符和收费条件下引用价格.我们记录了广告价格的版本观测台,并评估了其记录的可比性.由20个来源组成的故意注册表在1,315个卖家模型对中产生了1,625个报价记录.在2026年10月1日UTC记录,相应于10月2日. 一个与巴西相关的子集包含910个记录,包括来自巴西映射的卖家的100个BRL记录和来自葡萄牙市场的全球卖家的810美元记录. 卖家范围的身份映射保留了10个规范模型的110个报价; 1,205个非官方的卖家模型标识符仍然在策划的映射之外. 在匹配的报价中,26个需要价格审查. 在通过结构化或专用分类器提取的84美元的报价中,每个模型的输入和输出极限仅仅是官方报价所复制的.本子集中的31个非官方报价都缺乏明确的服务层次.该版本支持可复制的目录分析,同时保留未支持的同等服务价格排名.

### 英文原摘要

Public large language model API catalogs quote prices under heterogeneous currencies, identifiers, and charging conditions. We document a versioned observatory of advertised prices and evaluate the comparability of its records. A purposive registry of 20 sources yielded 1,625 offer records across 1,315 seller-model pairs. Fetch-start times were recorded on 1 October 2026 UTC, corresponding to 2 October. A Brazil-related subset contains 910 records, including 100 BRL records from Brazil-mapped sellers and 810 USD records from Portuguese-market global sellers. Seller-scoped identity mappings retain 110 offers for ten canonical models; 1,205 nonofficial seller-model identifiers remain outside the curated mapping. Of the matched offers, 26 require price review. Among 84 USD offers extracted by structured or dedicated parsers, every model's input and output extrema are reproduced by official offers alone. All 31 nonofficial offers in this subset lack explicit service tiers. The release supports reproducible catalog analysis while withholding unsupported equivalent-service price rankings.
